Questions

Do I need to pay for AI subscriptions?

Yes — MausBot runs on the agent accounts you already have (Claude, ChatGPT/Codex or Grok). If you have none, we will tell you honestly which single plan covers your case before you buy anything.

Is MausBot really free?

Yes. The app is open source under Apache 2.0 with no paid tier and no locked features. Our fee is only for designing and configuring your team.

Where does my data live?

On your machine, in MausBot's local data folder. Bots sign in to apps over OAuth and ask for approval before commands and file edits, per the rules we set with you.

What do you need from me?

Your computer (Mac, Windows or Ubuntu), your existing AI logins done by you during the guided session, and text replies. No passwords, no remote access to your accounts.

What if it is not for me?

If we cannot get your team running on your machine, the setup fee is refunded. Support is by text, replies within one business day.
